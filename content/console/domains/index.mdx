## Where to find domains

You can open domains from two places in the Console:

- **Agency Domains tab**: open your agency and go to **Domains** to see every domain in the agency.
- **Project Domains tab**: open a project and go to its **Domains** tab to see the domains for that project.

Click a domain in either list to open the domain's page, where you attach it to a project, see its DNS records and check its verification status.

## Primary and connected domains

A project can have more than one domain. When the site is published, it is served from its primary domain, and any other domains connected to the project automatically redirect to it.

You can change the primary domain at any time in the project's own domain settings. Only verified domains can be used.

## SSL/TLS certificates

Blutui automatically issues an SSL/TLS certificate for each domain once it's verified, so your site is served over HTTPS. There's nothing you need to do. The certificate encrypts traffic between visitors and your site and confirms the site's identity.
