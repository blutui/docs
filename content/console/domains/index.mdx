Domains are managed at the agency level in the Console. You add a domain once, attach it to a project and verify it with DNS records. Once the domain is verified and the project is published, visitors can reach the site on that domain.

## Primary and connected domains

A project can have more than one domain. When the site is published, it is served from its primary domain, and any other domains connected to the project automatically redirect to it.

You can change the primary domain at any time in the project's own domain settings. Only verified domains can be used.

## SSL/TLS certificates

Every domain needs an SSL/TLS certificate, which encrypts traffic between visitors and your site and confirms the site's identity. You can request a certificate for a domain from the project's domain settings.
