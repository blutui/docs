## Adding Your Domain(s) to Blutui

Now that you have built your project, it's time to connect your domain(s) to it.

1. In the Console, open your agency and go to **Domains**, then click **Add domain**.

2. Enter your domain name without `https://` (e.g., `example.com`) and click **Add domain**.

3. On the domain's page, select your project in the **Project** box and click **Confirm change**.

4. Add the A (or CNAME) and TXT records from the **DNS records** table at your DNS provider.

5. Wait for the automatic check, or click **Check DNS records** to verify the domain straight away.

For the full DNS record details, provider tips and troubleshooting, see [Connect a domain](/console/domains/connect-a-domain).

## Publish your site

![Publish project](https://cdn.blutui.com/uploads/assets/Dev/getting-started/publish-project.png)

Once you are happy with the changes to your project, you can publish your site to make it live.

From the Project overview page, click the **Publish** button.
