---
title: 'Connect a domain'
description: 'Add a domain in Blutui, attach it to a project, verify it with DNS records and make your site live.'
---

<Steps>
<Step>
### Add the domain in Blutui

1. In the Console, go to the **Domains** tab of your agency or of a project. See [Where to find domains](/console/domains#where-to-find-domains).
2. Click **Add domain**.
3. Under **Domain name**, type the domain without `https://` or a trailing slash, for example `example.com`.
4. Click **Add domain**.

This takes you to the domain's page, which shows a **Verification pending** banner.
</Step>

<Step>
### Attach it to a project

1. On the domain's page, find the **Project** box.
2. Open the dropdown, search for the project and select it.
3. Click **Confirm change** in the dialog that appears.
</Step>

<Step>
### Add the DNS records at your provider

The **DNS records** table at the bottom of the domain's page shows the exact records to add. Add them at your DNS provider (GoDaddy, Cloudflare, Namecheap, etc.):

- A root domain (`example.com`) needs an **A** record and a **TXT** record.
- A subdomain (`www.example.com`) needs a **CNAME** record and a **TXT** record.

<Callout type="warn">
Copy the records from the DNS records table rather than from the docs. The TXT record is unique to each domain.
</Callout>

See [DNS records](/console/domains/dns-records) for the record values, what to remove first, and tips for entering the Host field and using Cloudflare.
</Step>

<Step>
### Verify the domain

Blutui checks pending domains automatically every 2 minutes. To check straight away, click **Check DNS records** on the domain's page.

DNS changes usually take effect within minutes but can take up to 72 hours. Once the check passes, the domain shows as verified.

If the check fails, see [Troubleshooting](/console/domains/troubleshooting).
</Step>

<Step>
### Make the site live

The project must be [published](/console/site-settings/publishing) for the domain to serve the site.

If the project has more than one domain, choose which one is the [primary domain](/console/domains#primary-and-connected-domains) in the project's own domain settings.
</Step>
</Steps>
